### 🚀 About me

I started on the front line of customer experience at an e-commerce company, setting response-time
standards and keeping satisfaction at 4.5 stars. That taught me something I still use every day: the numbers
only matter if they change what people do next.

From there I built a Customer Success function from scratch at an edtech company (Venture Garden Group), growing it from one person
to a team of 15. By digging into survey and call feedback across 20,000+ students, we cut churn from **48% to
10.5% in a year**. Next I moved to fintech, where I led the onboarding of 50 enterprise partners for a North
American product launch (**+30% customers in 6 months**) and ran 70+ customer interviews that shaped the product.

To add the technical depth to that operational experience, I moved to Seattle for an **MS in Business Analytics
at the University of Washington** (2026, GPA 3.88, Beta Gamma Sigma). During the program I interned in Business
Intelligence & Analytics at **Keurig Dr Pepper**. There I analyzed 400,000+ SAP inventory records with SQL and Python
to re-slot a warehouse around SKU velocity. Order-picking efficiency rose **15% in 5 months**, processing time dropped
by 2 business days, and automated dashboards removed ~90 minutes of manual reporting every day.

I'm looking for data, BI, operations and customer analytics roles where I can bring both sides: the operator who
knows how the work really happens, and the analyst who can prove what to change.

### 🔭 Recent work

**[Warehouse Slotting & Inventory Analytics](https://github.com/YennyBaks/WAREHOUSE-SLOTTING)**: an interactive
Streamlit app that rebuilds my Keurig Dr Pepper internship project on 400,000 simulated SAP-style records. It does ABC classification
in SQL, maps 800 bins, flags misplaced stock, ranks a relocation plan and models the gain in picks per labor hour.
[Live demo](https://omoyeni-warehouse-slotting-dashboard.streamlit.app) · *Simulated data, no proprietary information.*
